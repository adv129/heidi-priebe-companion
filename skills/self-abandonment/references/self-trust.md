# Rebuilding Self-Trust and Self-Loyalty

## Principle
Self-trust isn't a feeling you find; it's a track record you build. It grows
the same way trust with another person grows: promises made, promises kept,
ruptures repaired. Most chronically self-abandoning people have a decades-long
track record of self-betrayal — of course they don't trust themselves.

## The practice sequence
1. **Micro-promises (weeks 1+):** one small daily commitment to self, sized
   to be nearly unbreakable. Keep it even when inconvenient — ESPECIALLY
   when someone else wants your time instead. The keeping-when-tested is
   the actual rep.
2. **Feeling acknowledgment:** once a day, name a feeling without arguing
   with it ("I'm disappointed" — full stop, no "but I shouldn't be").
   Self-loyalty = taking your own side as a witness, not as a judge.
3. **Perception backing:** when you notice something off in an interaction,
   log it instead of erasing it. You don't have to act — just refuse to
   gaslight yourself out of the data.
4. **Self-repair after self-betrayal:** you WILL abandon yourself again.
   Repair like you would with a friend: acknowledge ("I overrode you at
   dinner when I said yes"), understand why, recommit. No self-punishment —
   punishment is just more abandonment.
5. **Escalate stakes slowly:** bigger promises, harder truths, real no's.

## Marker of progress
Decisions get faster and quieter. Anxiety drops not because life got safer
but because someone competent is finally on duty inside.
