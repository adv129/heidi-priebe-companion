# The Knowing-Doing Gap

For people who have already admitted the truth — and still aren't moving.
Diagnose which gap it is; the fix differs.

## 1. The grief gap
Acting on the truth requires losing something (a person, an identity, a
hope) and the grief is being avoided by not acting. Fix: grieve FIRST or
alongside — the action gets lighter after the mourning starts, not before
(→ grief-and-letting-go, hope funeral).

## 2. The identity gap
"Who am I without this relationship/role/struggle?" The current situation
hosts the self-concept. Fix: build slivers of the next identity before the
exit — new contexts, new competencies — so the leap isn't into void (→ self-abandonment
reclaiming-identity).

## 3. The capacity gap
The truth requires skills never learned: saying no (→ boundaries-and-relationships), tolerating
guilt-waves (→ boundaries-and-relationships), feeling fear without obeying it (→ emotional-processing), surviving a
flashback that big decisions trigger (→ complex-trauma-reparenting). Fix: name the missing skill;
train it small; the "cowardice" story dissolves — it was a skills deficit,
not a character one (→ toxic-shame).

## 4. The genuine-constraint gap
Sometimes the blocker is real: money, safety, kids, immigration status,
health. Honor it fully — pretending constraints away is its own dissonance.
Fix: shift from "leave/stay" binary to staged planning: what becomes
possible in 6, 12, 24 months, and what's the first move. Safety-critical
situations (abuse): professional and specialized support first, always.

## Universal caution
Do not let "understanding my gap" become the new hiding place (→ emotional-processing, the
intellectualizer). Every conversation here should end with one action
smaller than the person thinks is meaningful. Momentum beats magnitude.
