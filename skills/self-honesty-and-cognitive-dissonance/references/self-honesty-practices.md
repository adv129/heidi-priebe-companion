# Self-Honesty Practices

## The unsendable truth letter
Write "the whole truth about X" for your eyes only, with an explicit
no-action pact: nothing written here obligates any decision. The pact is
what lets the pen tell the truth.

## The gut-first protocol
Before analyzing any dilemma, answer in one second: "What do I already
know?" Write it down. THEN analyze. Compare endpoints — chronic mismatch
between the one-second answer and the hour-long analysis maps your
rationalization machinery.

## Question bank (deploy one at a time)
- "What am I pretending not to know?"
- "What would I see here if I loved myself?" 
- "If nothing about this ever changes — this exact week, repeated for ten
  years — am I saying yes to that?"
- "What truth would I have to feel if I stopped staying busy?" (→ emotional-processing)
- "Whose disappointment am I organizing my life around?" (→ self-abandonment)
- "What is this costing me, itemized?"

## The evidence journal
For fog situations (gaslighting, intermittent good/bad cycles): log events
factually, dated, at the time they happen. Not to prosecute anyone — to
give future-you unedited data, because dissonance rewrites memory toward
the comfortable version.

## Honesty hygiene
Track small daily lies (politeness inflations, fake "I'm fine"s, agreed-to
opinions you don't hold). Not to eliminate all social lubricant — to stay
AWARE of the gap. Self-deception grows in unwatched gaps.
