# Earned Secure Attachment — The Path

## Definition
Security developed in adulthood despite insecure beginnings — through
corrective relational experiences, self-work, and (often) therapy. Research
and Priebe's framing agree: earned security is real security.

## The rough stage sequence
1. **Pattern awareness** — seeing the strategy while it runs.
2. **Origin compassion** — connecting strategy to childhood adaptation
   without self-pity or blame-forever (→ complex-trauma-reparenting for depth).
3. **Somatic literacy** — feeling activation/deactivation in the body in
   real time (→ emotional-processing).
4. **Behavioral interruption** — pausing the strategy, choosing a secure
   behavior while still feeling insecure. This stage feels WRONG. That's
   the sign it's working.
5. **Corrective experience accumulation** — direct asks that get met,
   ruptures that get repaired, closeness that doesn't punish.
6. **Grieving** — the childhood, the years lost to the pattern, the
   relationships it cost (→ grief-and-letting-go). Often the most skipped, most necessary stage.

## What secure actually looks like (behavior bank)
- States needs directly without protest or apology-spirals.
- Takes space WITH communication and a return time.
- Tolerates a partner's bad mood without absorbing or fixing it.
- Repairs after conflict; doesn't stonewall or punish.
- Finds consistency attractive rather than dull.
- Can be alone without emptiness and together without engulfment.

## Progress markers to reflect back to the person
Longer gap between trigger and reaction; anxiety that peaks and passes
without action; noticing the ick and questioning it; boredom with chaos.
Celebrate these explicitly — nervous systems learn from marked wins.
