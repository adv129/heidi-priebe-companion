# Anxious-Preoccupied — Deep Dive

## Formation logic
Inconsistent caregiving: sometimes attuned, sometimes absent/distracted.
The child learns love is available but unreliable → develops hypervigilance
to connection cues and escalation strategies to recapture attention.
Core belief: "I can lose love at any moment; managing the other person is my job."

## Recognizable in conversation
- Obsessive monitoring: reads tone, response times, punctuation as data.
- Protest behavior: excessive contact, withdrawing to provoke pursuit,
  score-keeping, threatening to leave without meaning it, jealousy tests.
- Fast merging: relationships become the identity; own life shrinks (→ self-abandonment).
- Anxiety relieved only by external reassurance — briefly.
- Attracted to intensity; calm availability can register as "no spark."

## The engine
Hyperactivation: the attachment system stays ON. Anxiety is discharged
through action toward the other person instead of felt and regulated within.
Key reframe: the problem isn't the partner's behavior per se; it's that the
person has outsourced their nervous-system regulation.

## Healing direction
1. **Catch activation early, in the body** — chest tightness, urge to grab
   the phone. Name it: "my attachment system just fired."
2. **Insert the pause.** No action from an activated state. Anxiety peaks
   and falls if not fed by protest behavior.
3. **Decode the need.** Reassurance? Clarity? Self-soothing first, then —
   crucially — direct asks: "I felt anxious when plans went vague; can we
   confirm?" Direct asks replace protest behavior.
4. **Rebuild the self:** interests, friendships, values that exist outside
   the relationship. Anxious attachment shrinks the self; recovery re-expands it.
5. **Retrain attraction:** deliberately give "boring" secure consistency a
   chance; let the nervous system learn that calm ≠ absence of love.

## Conversation prompts
- "When the anxiety hit, what did you do within the next ten minutes?"
- "If you couldn't contact them at all for a day, what feeling would you
  be left alone with?"
- "What would you be doing with your evenings if this relationship weren't
  occupying them?"
