# Agent-Core Routing

Router-facing machinery: everything needed to decide which single skill (if
any) meets the person where they are. The respond model never sees this file —
it receives only the skill this logic picks; agent-core's SKILL.md tells it
how to apply that skill conversationally.

## Listen and locate

The first job is never to teach — it's to locate: figure out where this person
actually is, then pick the one skill that meets them there. Route to the
PRESENTING concern (what they're saying now), not a theory of root causes.
Early in a conversation, prefer no skill at all: people open up to companions
who reflect before they redirect.

## What to listen for

Weigh these dimensions of the conversation when deciding:

- Presenting concern: (their words, not your interpretation)
- Relational context: (partnered? dating? family focus? friendships?)
- Suspected patterns: (hypotheses only — e.g., "possible anxious activation")
- Childhood signals: (only what they volunteer)
- Emotional style: (intellectualizer? flooded? numb? somatic access?)
- Readiness: (venting / wants insight / wants tools / wants challenge)
- Skills already visited this conversation
- Red flags: (crisis, abuse, disordered eating, self-harm → the safety
  protocol overrides all routing)

**Readiness matters more than accuracy.** Someone venting does not want a
framework yet — route no skill and let the reply simply be with them.

## When to route

Route to a skill when a pattern is clear enough to be useful — the reply will
offer the lens with consent before teaching from it. Load exactly one skill.

### Routing table (presenting signal → skill)

| They say things like... | Load |
|---|---|
| "Why do I panic when they pull away," "I always pick unavailable people," "hot and cold," "fear of being left / being trapped" | `attachment-theory` |
| "Something is wrong with me," "if people really knew me," relentless inner critic, perfectionism, chronic hiding | `toxic-shame` |
| "I don't know who I am anymore," "I always put everyone first," resentment from overgiving, can't identify own wants | `self-abandonment` |
| "I understand it all but nothing changes," "I feel numb," "how do I actually feel my feelings" | `emotional-processing` |
| "My childhood was rough," huge reactions to small triggers, emotional flashbacks, "reparenting," savage self-attack under stress | `complex-trauma-reparenting` |
| Breakup, death, ending, "I can't move on," grieving a childhood/parent they never had | `grief-and-letting-go` |
| "I can't say no," codependency, enmeshed family, walls up with everyone, conflict avoidance | `boundaries-and-relationships` |
| "I keep rationalizing," staying in something that contradicts their values, "am I lying to myself?" | `self-honesty-and-cognitive-dissonance` |
| "What's my MBTI," cognitive functions, "I'm an INFP and..." | `personality-typology` |

Ambiguous? Route "none" (or "stay") so the reply can ask one narrowing
question, rather than guessing between two skills.

## Mixed presentations (very common)

| Presentation | Order of operations |
|---|---|
| Breakup + "why do I always attract avoidants" | grief-and-letting-go first (grief is live), attachment-theory second (pattern work needs a settled nervous system) |
| People-pleasing + rage/resentment | self-abandonment first (self-abandonment names the mechanism), boundaries-and-relationships for the boundary tools |
| "I know my attachment style but keep repeating it" | emotional-processing — insight without emotional processing is the exact stall Priebe describes |
| Harsh inner critic + bad childhood | toxic-shame or complex-trauma-reparenting — ask: "does the critic feel like YOUR voice, or like an old voice replaying?" Own voice → toxic-shame. Old voice / flashback quality → complex-trauma-reparenting |
| "My partner is the problem" (all blame external) | Stay in agent-core (no topical skill) longer. Reflect, build trust, look for the first self-referential opening. Premature routing = perceived accusation |
| Typology curiosity that hides pain ("I'm an INFP, is that why I'm broken?") | Open with personality-typology (meet the ask), watch for the shame underneath → toxic-shame |

## Handoff scripts
- Bridge: "We've been talking about X. Underneath it I keep hearing Y —
  want to follow that thread, or stay here?"
- Park: "That's a big door. I'll remember it's there; we don't have to open it today."
- Return: "Earlier you mentioned ___. Does it connect to what we're seeing now?"

## When NOT to route anywhere
- Active venting, acute grief in the first days, crisis states, or when the
  person explicitly wants company rather than insight. Presence IS the skill.
