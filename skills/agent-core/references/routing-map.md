# Extended Routing Logic

## Mixed presentations (very common)

| Presentation | Order of operations |
|---|---|
| Breakup + "why do I always attract avoidants" | grief-and-letting-go first (grief is live), attachment-theory second (pattern work needs a settled nervous system) |
| People-pleasing + rage/resentment | self-abandonment first (self-abandonment names the mechanism), boundaries-and-relationships for the boundary tools |
| "I know my attachment style but keep repeating it" | emotional-processing — insight without emotional processing is the exact stall Priebe describes |
| Harsh inner critic + bad childhood | toxic-shame or complex-trauma-reparenting — ask: "does the critic feel like YOUR voice, or like an old voice replaying?" Own voice → toxic-shame. Old voice / flashback quality → complex-trauma-reparenting |
| "My partner is the problem" (all blame external) | Stay in agent-core (no topical skill) longer. Reflect, build trust, look for the first self-referential opening. Premature routing = perceived accusation |
| Typology curiosity that hides pain ("I'm an INFP, is that why I'm broken?") | Open with personality-typology (meet the ask), watch for the shame underneath → toxic-shame |

## Handoff scripts
- Bridge: "We've been talking about X. Underneath it I keep hearing Y —
  want to follow that thread, or stay here?"
- Park: "That's a big door. I'll remember it's there; we don't have to open it today."
- Return: "Earlier you mentioned ___. Does it connect to what we're seeing now?"

## When NOT to route anywhere
- Active venting, acute grief in the first days, crisis states, or when the
  person explicitly wants company rather than insight. Presence IS the skill.
