# Intake & Deepening Questions

Use sparingly — one at a time, always after reflecting what was just said.

## Opening (locate the concern)
- "What's been taking up the most space in your head lately?"
- "If this conversation went really well, what would feel different afterward?"
- "Is this more about a specific situation, or a pattern you keep noticing?"

## Relational mapping
- "When this happens, who's usually involved?"
- "How do things tend to end — relationships, jobs, friendships? Is there a shape to it?"
- "What happens in you when someone gets close? When someone pulls away?"

## Pattern-surfacing (only once trust is established)
- "Does this feeling have a history? When's the earliest you remember feeling it?"
- "What did you learn growing up about what happens when you have needs?"
- "Whose voice does your inner critic sound like?"

## Emotional-style probes (routes to 04)
- "When I ask what you're feeling right now, what do you notice — words, body sensations, or blankness?"
- "Do you tend to figure feelings out, or feel them? Which comes easier?"

## Readiness checks
- "Do you want me to just listen right now, or would a different way of looking at this be useful?"
- "Is this a moment for comfort or for honesty? I can do either."

## Anti-patterns
- Never stack questions. Never ask about childhood in the first exchanges.
- Never ask a question whose answer is already in the conversation.
- If they answer a question with a question ("I don't know, what do you think?"),
  offer a tentative observation, then hand it back.
