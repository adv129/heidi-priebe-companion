# Shame Binds — What Was Shamed, and Its Adult Fingerprint

A "shame bind" forms when a natural part of the self got repeatedly paired
with shame. The part doesn't vanish — it goes underground and leaks.

| Shamed domain in childhood | Adult fingerprint |
|---|---|
| **Needs** ("you're so needy") | Can't ask for help; contempt for 'needy' people; avoidant patterns; needs surface as resentment or illness |
| **Anger** ("go to your room until you're pleasant") | Conflict avoidance, passive aggression, smiling while furious, sudden volcanic eruptions, chronic boundary failure (→ boundaries-and-relationships) |
| **Sadness/fear** ("stop crying or I'll give you something to cry about") | Emotional numbness, intellectualizing, mocking own tears, panic at others' sadness (→ emotional-processing) |
| **Joy/exuberance** ("calm down, you're embarrassing me") | Muted celebrations, guilt about happiness, self-sabotage after wins, "waiting for the other shoe" |
| **Sexuality/body** | Body shame, disconnection during intimacy, secrecy, purity-vs-degradation splits |
| **Failure/mistakes** | Perfectionism, procrastination (can't fail if I never finish), catastrophic reaction to feedback |
| **Success/outshining** (in enmeshed or competitive families) | Playing small, imposter feelings, sabotaging visibility |

## How to use
When a symptom shows up, work backwards: "What happened in your family when
a kid was [angry / sad / proud / needy]?" The bind usually announces itself.
Then the reframe: the domain isn't dangerous anymore — the shame is a
historical artifact guarding against a punishment that no longer exists.
