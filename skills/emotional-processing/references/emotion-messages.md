# The Emotion Message Decoder

Every emotion = data + energy for a response. Suppress the data, and the
energy leaks sideways (symptoms, snapping, numbness, fatigue).

| Emotion | Message | Healthy response direction | Common leak when suppressed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sadness | Something that matters is lost/missing | Grieve; let it matter; seek comfort (→ grief-and-letting-go) | Numbness, anhedonia, chronic low energy |
| Anger | A boundary, value, or need was violated | Identify the line; assert or change it (→ boundaries-and-relationships) | Resentment, passive aggression, depression-as-anger-inward, sudden eruptions |
| Fear | Perceived threat | Assess: real vs. historical; protect or reality-check | Chronic anxiety, avoidance, control behaviors |
| Shame | Perceived exposure of a defect | Reality-check the 'defect'; safe disclosure (→ toxic-shame) | Perfectionism, hiding, rage, contempt |
| Guilt | I acted against my values | Repair, apologize, adjust behavior | Rumination without repair; or none (self-abandoners feel guilt for EXISTING — recalibrate → self-abandonment) |
| Loneliness | Connection need unmet | Reach out; also examine self-connection (→ self-abandonment) | Compulsive scrolling, parasocial substitution, isolation spiral |
| Envy | Someone has something I want | Extract the want; make it a goal | Bitterness, judgment, self-attack |
| Anxiety (chronic) | Often: a feeling underneath is being avoided | Ask "what would I feel if I stopped managing?" | The management IS the leak |

## Special notes
- **Anger work with self-abandoners:** they often can't feel anger at all
  (it was shamed → see 02 shame-binds). Resentment and fatigue are its
  fossils. Legitimizing anger is frequently the breakthrough emotion.
- **Mixed states:** most real moments are stacks (angry-because-hurt-
  because-scared). Peel one layer at a time; start with what's on top.
