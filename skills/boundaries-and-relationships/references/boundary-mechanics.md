# Boundary Mechanics

## Types (help the person locate WHICH boundary is failing)
Physical (touch, space) · Emotional (not absorbing/managing others'
feelings; not accepting blame-dumping) · Time/energy (availability,
workload) · Conversational (topics, tone: "I won't be yelled at") ·
Material (money, possessions) · Digital (response times, access).

## The formula
Statement of limit + your action, both in YOUR control:
- "I'm not discussing my weight. If it comes up again, I'll head out."
- "I can help Saturday, not tonight."
- "If the yelling starts, I'll end the call and we can try tomorrow."
No character verdicts ("you're toxic"), no demands you can't enforce
("you must stop"), no essays. Warm + short + repeatable.

## Holding under pushback (where it actually breaks)
Expect, in order: testing → guilt-tripping ("after everything I've done") →
escalation → sometimes smear or sulk. The broken-record technique: same
sentence, same calm tone, no new justifications. Each justification reopens
the negotiation.

## The internal work while holding
Tolerate: their disappointment, your guilt-wave, the silence after. This
tolerance is the muscle (→ emotional-processing for staying with the discomfort somatically).
Track wins by what YOU did, not how they reacted — their reaction was never
the controllable variable.

## Boundary audit prompt
"Where in your life are you currently doing something with resentment?"
Each answer = a boundary owed to yourself. Prioritize by resentment volume.
