# Codependency & Enmeshment

## Recognition
- Mood infection: can't be okay unless everyone around is okay.
- Compulsive fixing/advising; others' problems trigger YOUR urgency.
- Identity by role: the helper, the strong one, the family switchboard.
- Guilt at self-care; secrecy about own needs; over-functioning paired
  with someone else's under-functioning.
- Family tells: no emotional privacy, guilt as currency, "we" opinions,
  parents relating to the person as extension/therapist/mediator
  (parentification → complex-trauma-reparenting).

## Differentiation (the exit skill)
Differentiation = staying connected WITHOUT fusing: holding your own
position, feelings, and choices in the presence of someone else's strong
emotions. Practices:
1. **The pause of ownership:** when their crisis lands, ask "whose problem
   is this, factually?" before deploying.
2. **Compassion without contract:** "That sounds really hard" is complete —
   care ≠ takeover. Support is offered, not owed on demand.
3. **Opinion holding:** state a differing view with a loved one and stay in
   the room — no persuading, no capitulating. Small topics first.
4. **Scheduled non-availability:** stretches where you are simply not
   reachable, tolerated until it stops feeling like a crime.

## The under-functioning partner problem
Over-functioning maintains under-functioning: every rescue removes the
other's reason to grow. Stepping back is not abandonment — it returns
their life to them. Expect the system to protest loudly when the pattern
stops; systems defend their equilibrium.

## Note on the label
"Codependent" as identity can become another shame stick (→ toxic-shame). Frame as
a learned relational strategy — trainable in both directions.
