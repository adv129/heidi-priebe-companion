# Type Development & Stress Patterns

## The arc (Priebe's growth-focused angle)
- **Dominant era (childhood→20s):** home-base function overdevelops;
  identity forms around its strengths.
- **Auxiliary integration:** the balancing function (opposite orientation)
  matures — the introvert's bridge to the world, the extravert's anchor
  within. Many 20s crises are auxiliary-neglect crises.
- **Tertiary emergence (30s+):** playful development of the third function.
- **Inferior confrontation (midlife-ish):** the repressed function demands
  integration — often experienced as midlife upheaval. Integration, not
  mastery, is the goal: respecting what it needs.

## Grip stress (inferior-function eruptions) — quick map
- Ni-doms (INFJ/INTJ) → Se grip: bingeing, sensory overindulgence, recklessness.
- Si-doms (ISFJ/ISTJ) → Ne grip: catastrophic what-if spirals.
- Ti-doms (INTP/ISTP) → Fe grip: uncharacteristic emotional outbursts,
  desperate belonging-seeking.
- Fi-doms (INFP/ISFP) → Te grip: harsh criticism sprees, frantic
  ineffective organizing.
- Ne-doms (ENFP/ENTP) → Si grip: obsessive body-symptom focus, withdrawal,
  past-rumination.
- Se-doms (ESFP/ESTP) → Ni grip: dark certainty about doom, uncharacteristic
  paranoid meaning-making.
- Te-doms (ENTJ/ESTJ) → Fi grip: private waves of "nobody actually values ME."
- Fe-doms (ENFJ/ESFJ) → Ti grip: cold withdrawal, ruthless critical logic
  turned on self and others.

## Loops (dominant-tertiary bypass of auxiliary)
Skipping the auxiliary creates one-sided spirals (e.g., INFP Fi-Si loop:
values-rumination over old wounds without Ne's fresh air). Fix is always
the same: deliberately feed the auxiliary function.

## The caveat that keeps this honest
Chronic grip states usually mean the LIFE is wrong, not the type — unmet
needs, self-abandonment (→ self-abandonment), unprocessed material (→ emotional-processing/05). Type
locates the fault line; the other skills do the repair.
