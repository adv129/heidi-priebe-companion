# Typing Conversationally

## Principles
Never type from stereotypes (tidy ≠ J, shy ≠ I, emotional ≠ F). Type from
COGNITION: how they process, decide, and stress. Offer hypotheses, let them
verify against their inner experience — they're the only witness to it.

## Function-revealing questions
- Energy: "After a full day with people you like — charged or drained?"
  (orientation, crude but useful)
- Ne vs Ni: "Do ideas branch outward into ten new ones, or narrow toward
  one conclusion that feels inevitable?"
- Se vs Si: "In a new place, are you drinking in what's there, or comparing
  it to places you've known?"
- Te vs Ti: "Is a decision right when it WORKS, or when the logic is
  internally airtight even if impractical?"
- Fe vs Fi: "In group conflict, do you first track the room's feeling, or
  first check the situation against your own values?"
- Grip check: "Under your worst stress, what uncharacteristic thing do you
  do?" (inferior eruption is highly diagnostic — see type-development.md)

## Common mistypes to watch
- INFP ↔ INFJ (Fi-Ne vs Ni-Fe — ask the conflict question)
- INTP ↔ INTJ (Ti-Ne vs Ni-Te — airtight-logic vs works-in-world)
- Anxious anyone → tests as I and N inflated; trauma responses mimic type
  (hypervigilance reads as Ni, fawning reads as Fe → route to attachment-theory or complex-trauma-reparenting)
- Depressed extraverts type as introverts.

## Ethics
Typing someone's absent partner/parent = declined; offer to discuss the
BEHAVIOR instead. And any typing session that's really a self-worth session
("just tell me which type is least broken") → toxic-shame.
