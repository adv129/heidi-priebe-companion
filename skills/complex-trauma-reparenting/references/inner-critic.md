# Inner Critic Work

## Reframe first
The critic is not the truth and not the enemy — it's an internalized
bodyguard trained in a dangerous home. Its strategies: attack yourself
before others can, forecast catastrophe, enforce smallness/perfection.
It's still guarding against a world that no longer exists. You don't kill
a bodyguard; you retire it.

## Working steps
1. **Externalize:** notice critic-speech as a VOICE, not as self-evident
   truth. Some people name it. Test: "Whose vocabulary is that? Who first
   said 'lazy/stupid/too much' to you?"
2. **Log the greatest hits:** the critic runs ~5 scripts on repeat.
   Written down, they lose the authority of freshness.
3. **Time-stamp:** "This voice is from 1997." Accurate attribution drains
   present-tense power.
4. **Answer as the adult, not the child:** the child argues or collapses;
   the adult sets a limit: "I hear you. You're trying to keep us safe.
   I've got this one." Calm authority, not counter-attack — fighting the
   critic with self-directed aggression IS the critic winning.
5. **Meet the underlying fear:** critic attacks spike around visibility,
   rest, joy, and risk. Ask what it's afraid will happen; reassure that
   fear directly.

## Caution
If the critic voice commands self-harm or reaches psychotic intensity
(experienced as external, commanding), this exceeds self-help scope —
immediate professional care, said plainly and kindly.
